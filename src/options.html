Mail Checker for Bulwark

Enter the address of your Bulwark webmail. Your accounts are picked up from wherever you're logged in there.

Options

Which sound plays is set in Bulwark, per account. This volume only applies when the extension plays it: an open Bulwark tab that can play it uses its own volume.

Accounts

Always ignores mutes and Do Not Disturb set in Bulwark. Never can't silence an open Bulwark tab, which still follows Bulwark's own settings.

Getting two notifications for each email? Turn off push notifications in Bulwark's settings. This extension sends its own. Unofficial extension, not made by the Bulwark project. Needs the full edition of Bulwark; Lite isn't supported. Your lookout on the wall.
